Services

Two ways to work together.

A single-car session for an owner who wants their car shot right, or a recurring pipeline for a business that needs consistent content on schedule. Different clients, different paths — never the same pricing conversation.

For owners

Owner shoots

Book it, show up clean, drive home with a gallery.

One car, one session, built around the car you actually own — its paint, its stance, the lines worth walking around twice. No packages to pick from, no add-on menu, no upsell waiting at delivery. You tell me about the car; I handle where and when it gets shot.

01

Book

Tell me about the car and where you're based. We pick a location scouted for its paint and lines, and a time the light will cooperate.

02

Shoot

Show up clean and fuelled; the rest is on me. A session runs 60–90 minutes on location — no studio, no crew, no waiting around.

03

Delivery

First edited selects land in your inbox within 48 hours. Full gallery follows — yours to print, post, or use to sell the car.

What's included

  • Location scouted to the car, not the calendar
  • 25–40 fully edited frames, delivered high-res and web-sized
  • First selects back within 48 hours of the shoot
  • Full usage rights — print it, post it, sell the car with it

The pricing promise

You pay what the work is worth to you — after you've seen it.

No deposit, no invoice before the gallery lands. You look at the photos of your car first, then you decide what they were worth. Most clients choose $50–100. It's a promise I can make because of how the photos come out.

For business

Commercial & fleet

Content that keeps up with inventory that moves.

Dealerships, rental fleets, and shops need listing and marketing photography on a schedule they can plan around — consistent framing, consistent grade, predictable turnaround. Every vehicle photographed the same way, every time, so a listing page looks like one showroom instead of twenty phone cameras. Your team stops thinking about the photos and gets back to moving units.

Built for the business

  • Listing and marketing sets, shot to a fixed spec
  • Recurring weekly or per-delivery scheduling
  • Consistent framing and color grade across the whole lot
  • Commercial usage rights included
  • Faster turnaround than waiting on a full production crew

Custom-quoted
